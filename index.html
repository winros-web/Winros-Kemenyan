Every grain of gum benzoin begins with a single cut in the bark. The resin seeps out milky white, hardens on the trunk, then is picked one by one by local farmers. What remains is a sweet, balsamic aroma that people have burned for centuries.

Origin

How the resin is born.

  1. 01The Styrax tree

    Benzoin trees grow in the highland forests of Sumatra.

  2. 02Tapped

    A local farmer cuts small notches in the bark with a tapping knife.

  3. 03The resin flows

    Thick, sticky, milky-white resin seeps from every notch.

  4. 04It hardens on the trunk

    The resin stays on the trunk for weeks until it sets into tears.

  5. 05Picked by local farmers

    Farmers pick the hardened tears one by one into a basket.

  6. 06Sorted into grades

    Every harvest is sorted by hand by size and colour.

The three ingredients

Natural ingredients. Global possibilities.

Selected natural ingredients from Indonesia for fragrance, incense, leather tanning and botanical innovation.

01
Menyan Madu · Gum Benzoin

The sweet side of nature.

A naturally warm, balsamic aroma with a distinctive sweet character.

  1. Aroma profileSweet and balsamic, vanilla-like, depending on species and grade.
  2. Fragrance materialUsed in perfumery as a fixative. BRIN researchers found high-value imported perfumes using Indonesian benzoin.
  3. Distinctive chemistrySumatra benzoin contains cinnamic acid as well as benzoic acid; Siam benzoin does not.
  4. Recognised worldwideExported at 5,000–6,000 tonnes a year to around 60 countries; France and Spain are among the buyers.

02
Menyan Bakar · Gum Benjamin

Ancient heritage. Timeless fragrance.

A natural aromatic resin, treasured across generations, for incense and fragrance.

  1. Incense across culturesA main component of Orthodox and Catholic church incense; burned on charcoal across the Arabian Peninsula and in Hindu temples in India.
  2. Bakhoor ingredientMade into bakhoor and blended incense in Arab countries and the Horn of Africa.
  3. Sought after in the Middle EastIndonesia's Ministry of Trade lists benzoin among Indonesian products in demand at trade fairs in Saudi Arabia.
  4. More than incenseAlso used as a raw material by the skincare and pharmaceutical industries, for example as a film former and taste masker.

03
Gambir · Uncaria gambir

Botanical power. Industrial potential.

Plant-derived tannins and catechins, rooted in Indonesia. 80% of the world's gambier comes from here.

  1. Vegetable tanninA plant-based material for leather tanning.
  2. Rich in catechinGambier from North Sumatra is recorded at 70–85% catechin. Actual content follows lab testing.
  3. One ingredient, many industriesCatechin is used by the pharmaceutical, cosmetics, batik and paint industries; gambier is also used as a dye.
  4. Steady demandIndia is the largest buyer (13–14 thousand tonnes a year), followed by Japan, China, Pakistan, Bangladesh and parts of Europe.

Form, grade, catechin content, packing, MOQ and price: for further details, please contact our sales team directly.
